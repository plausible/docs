---
title: Reset your site data
description: "How to reset all collected stats for a site in Plausible Analytics. The site stays in your account but all historical data is permanently cleared."
---

import useBaseUrl from '@docusaurus/useBaseUrl';

Within the **Danger zone** section of [your website settings](website-settings.md), find **Reset stats** and click **Reset [your domain] stats**. Confirm the reset when prompted.

<div class="browser screenshot-padded">
  <img alt="Plausible site settings: Reset stats removes collected analytics while keeping the site configuration" src={useBaseUrl('img/plausible-reset-stats.webp')} width="1622" height="340" decoding="async" />
</div>

This permanently removes all the stats you've collected until that point while keeping the site and its configuration in your account. The separate **Delete site** action removes both the stats and the site settings.

Stats for a single page or specific custom property values can't be deleted on their own once they've been recorded.
