## How Plausible is typically classified in security reviews

Plausible does not collect personal data or track individual users. There are no cookies, no cross-site tracking and no persistent user identifiers. Because of this, Plausible is typically classified as a low-risk service in vendor security assessments and GDPR impact analyses.

The specific reasons it falls into this category:

- No personal data is collected. Plausible does not store IP addresses, device fingerprints or any other persistent identifiers.
- No cookies are set. There is nothing to consent to and no cookie banner is required. If you run a banner for other tools, Plausible can [load outside it](consent-management-platforms.md).
- All visitor data is processed and stored in the EU on servers owned and operated by European companies, and never leaves the EU.
- No data is shared with or sold to third parties.
- A DPA is in place automatically for all customers, covering GDPR processor obligations.

## Letting visitors opt out

If your privacy policy needs to offer visitors a way to object to analytics, you can add a ready-made opt-out page to your site and link to it from the policy. [See how](excluding-localstorage.md).

## Keeping campaign parameters out of your stats

Plausible records UTM tags and the address of the referring page by default. If your review needs them left out, you can remove them in the tracking snippet before anything is sent. [See how](stop-tracking-utm-tags.md).
